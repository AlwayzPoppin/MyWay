Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026 • Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Welcome to MyWay ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy, personal safety, and data security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how MyWay collects, uses, protects, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application and location services.

Core Privacy Promise: MyWay is built strictly for family and private circle safety. We NEVER sell your personal or location data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide real-time family location tracking, crash detection, and safety navigation, MyWay collects the following categories of information:

Data Category Specific Data Elements Primary Purpose
Location Data Precise GPS coordinates, speed, heading, altitude, and timestamp (Foreground & Background). Live circle member location sharing, geofence arrival/departure alerts, turn-by-turn navigation, and SOS emergency beaconing.
Account Info Display name, email address, profile photo URL (optional), and Firebase User ID (UID). Account authentication, profile identification within your private circles, and account recovery.
Motion & Sensors Accelerometer, gyroscope, and device motion data (Processed locally). Automated vehicular crash impact detection and drive behavior analysis.
Circle Communications In-app circle messages, check-ins, and safety alert broadcasts. Facilitating private communication and emergency responses among members of your shared circles.
Device & Diagnostics Battery percentage, device model, OS version, push notification tokens (FCM), and crash logs. Low-battery family alerts, push notifications for circle safety events, and app stability optimization.

2. Background Location Usage

MyWay requires access to Background Location ("Allow all the time") to deliver core safety functionality even when the app is minimized or your phone screen is turned off:

  • Family Circle Safety: Allows designated family circle members to view your real-time location.
  • Automated Geofence Alerts: Triggers arrival and departure notifications (e.g., "Arrived at School" or "Left Work").
  • Emergency SOS & Crash Response: Immediately broadcasts precise distress coordinates to your emergency contacts upon high-G vehicle impact or SOS trigger.

Users can adjust location tracking accuracy modes (Active, Balanced, Battery Saver, or Ghost Mode) at any time directly in the app settings.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not share your personal information except in the following limited situations:

  • Within Your Private Circles: Your live location, speed, battery level, and messages are shared only with users whom you have explicitly joined or invited into a private circle.
  • Service Providers (Sub-processors): We utilize trusted cloud infrastructure providers to operate the service:
    • Google Firebase: Authentication, encrypted cloud database, push notification delivery (FCM), and serverless functions.
    • OpenStreetMap / MapLibre: Map rendering and navigation vector data.
  • Legal & Emergency Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or in emergency situations to protect physical safety.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured with industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. Data at rest is encrypted within Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure. Sensitive device sensor processing for crash detection is performed locally on your physical device.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Location Pause / Ghost Mode: Temporarily freeze or blur your location in circles via app settings.
  • Leave Circles: Disconnect immediately from circle location sharing.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can delete your account and associated location history at any time directly in Settings > Privacy > Delete Account or by contacting support at support@mywaygps.com. Upon request, all personal data is permanently wiped from our active databases within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

When MyWay is used to safeguard children or dependents within a family circle, accounts must be established under parental or legal guardian supervision and consent.
