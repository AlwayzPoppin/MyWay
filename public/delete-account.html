Account & Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and provide simple, transparent methods to permanently delete your MyWay account and all associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

📱 Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account immediately from inside the MyWay application:

  • Open the MyWay app.
  • Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top header.
  • Scroll down to the Privacy & Security section.
  • Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.

Your account credentials, circle memberships, and location traces are immediately deleted from active servers.

✉️ Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit a deletion request via email:

Send an email from your registered MyWay account email address to support@mywaygps.com with the subject line Delete My Account.

📧 Submit Deletion Request via Email

Our privacy team will process your request and permanently wipe your account and all associated records within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted?

When an account deletion is executed, the following data categories are permanently purged:

  • Account Profile: Email address, display name, profile photo, and Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Location Data: All real-time and historical GPS location logs, route records, and speed data.
  • Circle Data: Private family circle memberships, circle admin roles, and custom geofence zones.
  • Messages & Alerts: In-app circle chat history, check-in pings, and emergency SOS event records.
  • Device Tokens: Push notification tokens (FCM) and device analytics.

Data Retention Policy

No personal or location information is retained following account deletion. Any aggregated analytics that cannot be linked back to an individual user are dissociated and anonymized.
